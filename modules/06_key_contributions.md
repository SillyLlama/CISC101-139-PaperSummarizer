# Module 6: Key contributions summarizer

## Responsibilities
- **Highlight 3–5 contributions** strictly supported by the paper.
- **Scope:** Architecture, training efficiency, performance, theoretical/computational analysis.

## Inputs
- **Section summaries** and **paper text.**

## Outputs
- **Contributions list:** Each item a single sentence or two, precise and non-redundant.

## Selection criteria
- **Novelty:** Firsts or replacements of prior approaches.
- **Empirical results:** State-of-the-art claims with metrics as reported.
- **Efficiency:** Training time/parallelization properties stated in the paper.
- **Method:** Architectural innovations (e.g., multi-head self-attention, positional encoding).

## Pseudocode
- **Scan summaries** for “new,” “state-of-the-art,” numeric metrics (e.g., BLEU), and architectural claims.
- **Rank and select** 3–5 items that cover method, results, and efficiency.
- **Compose concise items** without duplicating final integrated summary sentences.
